# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun)
[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge2.svg)](https://make.fun)

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"**
([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb)).

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single monologue of exactly 100 words to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set). Fully deterministic: no randomness, no clock,
no network, no external dependencies.

The monologue is spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired —
a dark lord drowning in nine centuries of paperwork. He wrote the prophecy
himself, paid a scribe to write it, then taxed the scribe. The hero arrives
with the blessed sword; the lord just wants to finish one page of his ledger
first. The tiredness is never announced — it leaks out sideways, in the chair
with a biting spring, a quiet "please", and the last line's sting.

## How it was chosen

Three hand-written candidates were scored against the judge brief's criteria
in the judge's own priority order (on-subject ~100 words; tiredness felt in
rhythm, never announced; a specific villain with history and scheme;
speakable in one breath with a turn in the last line; every word spent).
The winner — "The Ledger", a bureaucrat dark lord facing his captive hero —
scored highest (24/25) and was revised once more to trade a redundant word
for a concrete, personal detail ("your grandmother's blessed sword").

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
