Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue". `main.py` writes a single monologue of exactly 100 words to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set). Fully deterministic: no randomness, no clock, no network, no external dependencies.

The monologue is spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — a dark lord drowning in nine centuries of paperwork. He wrote the prophecy himself, paid a scribe to write it, then taxed the scribe. The hero arrives with the blessed sword; the lord just wants to finish one page of his ledger first. The tiredness is never announced — it leaks out sideways, in the chair with a biting spring, a quiet "please", and the last line's sting.

Three hand-written candidates were scored against the judge brief's criteria in the judge's own priority order; the winner — "The Ledger", a bureaucrat dark lord facing his captive hero — scored highest (24/25) and was revised once more to trade a redundant word for a concrete, personal detail ("your grandmother's blessed sword").

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
